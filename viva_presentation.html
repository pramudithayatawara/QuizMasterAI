QuizMasterAI: Intelligent Dual-Phase Assessment & Adaptive Battle Engine

An on-premise, privacy-preserving assessment system with competitive multiplayer Battle Mode and a consolidated Results-driven Leaderboard.

Institution: Rajarata University of Sri Lanka (RUSL) • Domain: Artificial Intelligence & EdTech • Core System: Dual-Phase AI Pipeline
01. Project Overview

Dual-Phase AI Approach for Assessment

QuizMasterAI decouples assessment generation into question formulation and cognitive distractor calibration, powered by competitive multiplayer gamification.

🧠

Dual-Phase AI Pipeline

Separates generation into Contextual Stem Formulation and Difficulty/Distractor Calibration to eliminate hallucinations and trivial options.

⚔️

Battle Mode Entity

Facilitates competitive assessment where multiple users 'Join' via a unique Battle_ID, with real-time scoring and Winner_ID tracking.

🏆

Consolidated Leaderboard

Consolidated entity automatically 'Updated' by Results to dynamically rank users, award XP, and unlock achievement badges.

02. Problem & Proposed Solution

Transforming Traditional Assessment Creation

Overcoming manual authoring bottlenecks and external API dependencies through an on-premise neural pipeline.

⚠️

The Problem

  • Laborious & Slow: Formulating valid questions and plausible distractors manually takes extensive educator hours.
  • Lack of Engagement: Traditional static quizzes lack engagement and competitive multi-user peer collaboration.
  • Cloud API Vulnerabilities: Relying on OpenAI/Gemini tokens incurs recurring costs, network latency, and student privacy risks.
💡

The Proposed Solution

  • Dual-Phase AI Engine: Guaranteed 1 correct answer and 3 close distractors with zero paid external API keys.
  • Competitive Battle Arena: Multiplayer sessions matching students by difficulty, identifying winners dynamically.
  • Automated Results Pipeline: Instant synchronization between exam results, battle victories, and global leaderboards.
03. System Architecture

Two Main Phases & Competitive Entities

Decoupled neural generation architecture integrated with multi-user Battle & Leaderboard database entities.

⚙️
Phase 1

Stem & Ground Truth Formulation

  • Conditioned on domain concepts: Programming, Mathematics, Science.
  • Extracts concept relationships and generates unambiguous question stems.
  • Validates ground truth targets with 100% semantic verification.
🎛️
Phase 2

Distractor Synthesis & Results Calibration

  • Generates 3 plausible distractors from the target concept space.
  • Calibrates difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard) and balances A-D keys.
  • Triggers downstream Results Entity updates directly into the Leaderboard.

Key Breakthrough

Eliminating ambiguous syntactic templates and adopting latent difficulty conditioning boosted overall sequence accuracy from 81.5% to 92.4%.
